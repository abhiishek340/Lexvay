Part A. Degree. An applicant shall hold a graduate degree in counseling of at least 60 semester credit hours.
Part B. Ethics. The degree shall include coursework in professional ethics and the legal standards of counseling practice.
Part C. Human growth. The degree shall include coursework in human growth and development.
Part D. Group counseling. The degree shall include coursework in group counseling.
Part E. Internship. The applicant shall have completed at least 600 clock hours of supervised internship.
Part F. Accreditation. The program shall hold accreditation from a counseling accrediting body recognized by the board.
Part G. Diagnosis. The degree shall include coursework in the diagnosis of mental and emotional disorders.
Part H. Addictions. The degree shall include coursework in addictions counseling.
