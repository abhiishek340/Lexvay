Demo rule set - Utah (fictional). Fictional. Prototype data. Not the law of any state.
Section 1. Degree. An applicant shall hold a graduate degree in counseling of at least 60 semester credit hours.
Section 2. Ethics. The degree shall include coursework in professional ethics and legal standards for counselors.
Section 3. Human growth. The degree shall include coursework in human growth and development across the lifespan.
Section 4. Diagnosis. The degree shall include coursework in the diagnosis and treatment planning of mental disorders.
Section 5. Internship. The applicant shall have completed at least 600 clock hours of supervised internship.
Section 6. Accreditation. The program shall be accredited by a counseling program accrediting body recognized by the board.
Section 7. Group counseling. The degree shall include coursework in group counseling theory and practice.
Section 8. Addictions. The degree shall include coursework in substance use and addictive disorders.
Section 9. State law. The applicant shall complete a course on the Utah Mental Health Professional Practice Act (fictional).
