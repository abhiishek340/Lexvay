Demo rule set - California (fictional). Fictional. Prototype data. Not the law of any state.
Article 1. Degree. An applicant shall hold a graduate degree in counseling of at least 60 semester credit hours.
Article 2. Ethics. The degree shall include coursework in professional ethics and law for counselors.
Article 3. Human growth. The degree shall include coursework in human growth and development across the lifespan.
Article 4. Crisis and trauma. The degree shall include coursework in crisis and trauma counseling.
Article 5. Practicum. The applicant shall have completed at least 100 clock hours of supervised practicum.
Article 6. Psychopharmacology. The degree shall include coursework in psychopharmacology.
Article 7. Telehealth. The applicant shall complete training in the provision of counseling by telehealth.
Article 8. Assessment. The degree shall include coursework in assessment, appraisal and testing.
