Demo rule set - Colorado (fictional), revision 2. Fictional. Prototype data. Not the law of any state.
Rule 1. Degree. An applicant shall hold a graduate counseling degree of at least 60 semester credit hours.
Rule 2. Ethics. The degree shall include coursework in professional ethics and legal issues in counseling.
Rule 3. Crisis and trauma. The degree shall include coursework in crisis intervention and trauma-informed counseling.
Rule 4. Practicum. The applicant shall have completed at least 100 clock hours of supervised practicum.
Rule 5. Internship. The applicant shall have completed at least 700 clock hours of supervised internship.
Rule 6. Career development. The degree shall include coursework in career development and career counseling.
Rule 7. Research. The degree shall include coursework in research methods and program evaluation.
Rule 8. Assessment. The degree shall include coursework in assessment and testing in counseling.
Rule 9. Psychopharmacology. The degree shall include coursework in psychopharmacology for counselors.
