# COUN 610 Human Growth and Development
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
The course surveys theories of human growth and development across the lifespan, including cognitive, emotional, social, and moral development from infancy through late adulthood.
Students connect developmental theory to counseling practice with clients of every age.

## Learning outcomes
Compare major theories of lifespan development.
Apply developmental knowledge to case conceptualization.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-4 Infancy and childhood. Weeks 5-8 Adolescence. Weeks 9-12 Adulthood. Weeks 13-14 Late adulthood and end of life.
