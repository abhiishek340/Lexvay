# Northfield University — Clinical Experience Handbook
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27.

## Practicum
The practicum requires a minimum of 100 clock hours, of which at least 40 are direct client contact.
Students receive one hour of individual supervision each week and ninety minutes of group supervision.

## Internship
The internship requires a minimum of 600 clock hours, of which at least 240 are direct client contact.
Internship placements are approved by the clinical coordinator and supervised by a licensed clinician.

## Site requirements
Sites provide clients representative of the community and access to a range of counseling services.
---
## Supervision and evaluation
Site supervisors hold an active clinical license and have at least two years of post-licensure experience.
Students are evaluated at midterm and at the end of each term using the clinical competency scale.
