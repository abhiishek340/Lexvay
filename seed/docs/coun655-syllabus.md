# COUN 655 Psychopharmacology for Counselors
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Elective course.

## Course description
This elective introduces the classes of psychotropic medications, their mechanisms and side effects, and the counselor's role in collaborating with prescribing providers.
The course does not prepare counselors to prescribe.

## Learning outcomes
Describe the major classes of psychotropic medications and their common side effects.
Communicate with prescribers about a shared client.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-5 Medication classes. Weeks 6-10 Side effects and adherence. Weeks 11-14 Collaboration.
