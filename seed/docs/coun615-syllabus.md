# COUN 615 Diagnosis and Treatment Planning
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
Students learn to formulate diagnoses using the current diagnostic and statistical manual, write treatment plans, and recognize the limits of diagnostic categories.
Cultural and contextual factors in diagnosis are examined throughout.

## Learning outcomes
Formulate a differential diagnosis from a clinical interview.
Write a measurable treatment plan linked to the diagnosis.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-3 Diagnostic systems. Weeks 4-10 Major diagnostic categories. Weeks 11-14 Treatment planning.

## Assessment and grading
Participation 10%, reflection papers 30%, midterm 25%, final project 35%.
