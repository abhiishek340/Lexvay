# COUN 640 Assessment and Testing in Counseling
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
Students learn the selection, administration, scoring, and interpretation of assessment instruments used in clinical mental health counseling, including principles of validity and reliability.
Ethical and cultural considerations in testing are addressed in every unit.

## Learning outcomes
Select an appropriate instrument for a stated clinical question.
Interpret standardized scores for a client in plain language.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-4 Measurement principles. Weeks 5-10 Instruments. Weeks 11-14 Interpretation and reporting.
