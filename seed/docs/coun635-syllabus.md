# COUN 635 Research and Program Evaluation
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
The course covers research design, statistics for counselors, program evaluation methods, and the critical reading of counseling outcome research.
Students design a small evaluation of a counseling program.

## Learning outcomes
Critique a published counseling outcome study.
Design a program evaluation with measurable outcomes.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-5 Research design. Weeks 6-9 Statistics. Weeks 10-14 Program evaluation.
---
## Assessment and grading
Participation 10%, reflection papers 30%, midterm 25%, final project 35%.

## Policies
Students follow the university academic integrity policy and the program's professional dispositions policy.
