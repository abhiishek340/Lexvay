# COUN 605 Professional Orientation and Ethical Practice
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
Students examine the ACA Code of Ethics, state licensing law, confidentiality, duty to warn, and the legal standards that govern clinical mental health counselors.
The course introduces the professional identity of the counselor and the history of the counseling profession.

## Learning outcomes
Apply an ethical decision-making model to counseling dilemmas.
Explain the legal standards for confidentiality, privileged communication, and duty to warn.

## Schedule
Week 1 Professional identity. Week 2 Ethical codes. Week 3 Legal standards and confidentiality. Week 4 Duty to warn.
Weeks 5-14 Ethics across settings, advocacy, and professional organizations.
---
## Assessment and grading
Participation 10%, reflection papers 30%, midterm 25%, final project 35%.

## Policies
Students follow the university academic integrity policy and the program's professional dispositions policy.
