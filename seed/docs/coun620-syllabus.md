# COUN 620 Crisis, Trauma and Disaster Response
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
The course prepares students to respond to crises, trauma, and disasters, including suicide risk assessment, psychological first aid, and trauma-informed counseling.
Students practice crisis intervention skills in simulated sessions.

## Learning outcomes
Conduct a suicide risk assessment and safety plan.
Describe the principles of trauma-informed care.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-4 Crisis theory and intervention models. Weeks 5-9 Trauma and its treatment. Weeks 10-14 Disaster mental health.
