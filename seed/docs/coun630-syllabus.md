# COUN 630 Career Development and Counseling
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
This course examines the role of work in people's lives and the counselor's part in supporting life planning and transitions.
One class session introduces career development theory and the use of career assessment instruments.

## Learning outcomes
Describe how work and life roles interact across the lifespan.
Support a client through a work-related transition.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-6 Work and meaning. Week 7 Career development theory and instruments. Weeks 8-14 Transitions and life planning.
