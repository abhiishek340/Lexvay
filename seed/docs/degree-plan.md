# Northfield University — Degree Plan
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, catalog year 2026-27.

## Program requirements
The program requires the completion of 60 semester credit hours.
All students complete the eight required core courses, the clinical sequence, the capstone sequence, and two electives.

## Required core courses (3 credits each)
COUN 605 Professional Orientation and Ethical Practice
COUN 610 Human Growth and Development
COUN 615 Diagnosis and Treatment Planning
COUN 620 Crisis, Trauma and Disaster Response
COUN 625 Group Counseling
COUN 630 Career Development and Counseling
COUN 635 Research and Program Evaluation
COUN 640 Assessment and Testing in Counseling

## Clinical sequence
COUN 660 Counseling Practicum and Internship (9 credits across three terms).

## Capstone sequence
COUN 670 Counseling Capstone (6 credits)
COUN 675 Advanced Clinical Internship (9 credits across three terms)
COUN 680 Applied Research Project (6 credits)

## Electives (choose two, 3 credits each)
COUN 645 Marriage, Couple and Family Counseling
COUN 650 Addictions Counseling
COUN 655 Psychopharmacology for Counselors
Additional electives are approved by the program director.
