# COUN 650 Addictions Counseling
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Elective course.

## Course description
This elective covers the etiology of substance use and addictive disorders, screening and assessment of addictions, and evidence-based treatment approaches across the lifespan.
Students study motivational interviewing and relapse prevention.

## Learning outcomes
Screen for substance use disorders with a validated instrument.
Describe evidence-based treatment approaches for addictions.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-4 Etiology and models. Weeks 5-9 Screening and assessment. Weeks 10-14 Treatment.
