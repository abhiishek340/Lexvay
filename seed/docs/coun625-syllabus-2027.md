# COUN 625 Group Counseling
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2027-28. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
Students study group dynamics, group leadership styles, stages of group development, and the ethical and legal considerations specific to group counseling practice.
The revised course adds a unit on facilitating groups delivered through video platforms.

## Learning outcomes
Lead a counseling group through its stages of development.
Apply group ethics to in-person and video-delivered groups.

## Schedule
Weeks 1-3 Group dynamics. Weeks 4-8 Leadership and stages. Weeks 9-12 Experiential group. Weeks 13-14 Video-delivered groups.
