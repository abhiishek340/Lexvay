## UI3-2 — Changes, Audit trail, Evidence report, Register and People in design v3

Branch `ui3-records`, decisions D-510 to D-519 in `docs/decisions/UI3-2.md`. Frontend only.

### Spec points
1. **Changes and the audit trail** (D-510, D-511, D-513): plain logs grouped by day under meta headings, one sentence per
   40 px row with the time in meta at the right, the detail opening below the row; timelines, dots, avatars and the
   sunken detail boxes removed, with PrimeNG's Timeline and Avatar and their token sets. Shared `.lx-log-*` classes and
   `ui/days.ts`. Changes' re-run is a row action; Blocked and Open are terms with `tooltipEvent="both"`.
2. **Shared skeleton** (D-512): `ui/skeleton-rows.ts`, used by every screen of this item.
3. **Evidence report** (D-514 to D-517): the 816 px sheet; the states as the shared tab row; Check a file, Download
   evidence package and Download PDF in the title row; the package requested, polled ("Preparing package…") and
   downloaded from F15's endpoints, only for a signed determination with a ready report, with one meta line saying why
   it is unavailable otherwise; F14's check dialog restyled. No spinners.
4. **Register and People** (D-518, D-519): tables under a title row and a meta line (the count), the tab's or screen's
   action in the title row, filters as one row of Selects, no avatars, no menu icons, no sub-lines.
5. **Setup page** (D-519): the sign-in column with 16 px icons and grid spacing.
6. **Verify (SEC-3)**: not built; PR #53 was still open at the start and at the final merge of main.

### Tests
- Unit: `ui/days.spec.ts` (moved from the audit view), `change-row.spec.ts` (sentences, names, blockers),
  `report-downloads.spec.ts` (the unavailable line), audit view spec without avatars; icon registration updated (Changes,
  the audit trail and People register no icons of their own).
- End-to-end: the package flow (disabled with its reason on a draft and before the PDF, then a ZIP named for the
  program, state and day, starting `PK`); the Changes log (sentences, the disclosure, the re-run); the report's state
  tabs in place of the Select in every spec that used it; the register's count line; axe at 1440 and 400 px as before.
- Screenshots: the UI3-2 test in `screens.spec.ts` (replacing UI2-3's).
