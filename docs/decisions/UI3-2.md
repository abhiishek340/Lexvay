### UI3-2 — Changes, Audit trail, Evidence report, Register and People in design v3 (D-510 to D-519)

**D-510 — One log pattern for Changes and the audit trail, in `styles.css`.** DESIGN.md asks both for "plain lists grouped
by day under meta headings; one sentence per row, the time in meta at the right, detail below the row". The two screens
share the shape, so it is one set of global classes beside `.lx-table` (D-491), not two local copies: `.lx-log-day`,
`.lx-log-heading` (a 40 px meta heading on a line-strong rule, the table header's rhythm), `.lx-log-rows`, `.lx-log-row`
(a line hairline below), `.lx-log-line` (a 40 px line: 10 px above and below a 20 px sentence), `.lx-log-toggle`,
`.lx-log-time` and `.lx-log-details`. The sentence is the row's disclosure button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`);
its `::after` stretches over the whole line, so a click anywhere on the row opens it, and anything else on the line (a
re-run, a term) is positioned above that area. The line's hover is `sunken`, bleeding 12 px past the text on both sides
so the sentence never touches the edge of its own highlight. No caret: the hover, the pointer and the expanded state
carry the affordance, and a column of chevrons was exactly the "icon on every row" v2 failure. Details are a two-column
definition list (labels in meta at 160 px, values in Body), no fill, rising 4 px into place with the route's keyframes.
Timeline dots, connectors, avatars and the inset sunken boxes are gone, and with them PrimeNG's Timeline and Avatar in
both chunks; `theme/timeline-tokens.ts` and `theme/avatar-tokens.ts` are deleted with their last callers. Both screens
keep one 880 px measure for the title row, the filters and the log, so the times sit under the end of the title row.

**D-511 — Grouping by day is one generic function.** `ui/days.ts` `groupByDay(items, at, now)` replaces the audit trail's
own `groupByDay` (moved, not copied, with its tests to `ui/days.spec.ts`); Changes groups by `createdAt` with it. Audit
rows lost `initials` and `actorKind`, which only fed the avatar and its fallback icon; the actor's name ("System",
"Former user" included) stays and is set in 500 at the start of the sentence.

**D-512 — The shared skeleton is a component.** UI3-0 left five local `.lx-skeleton` copies of the same idea. `ui/skeleton-
rows.ts` is the D-496 pattern once: a host that is one named `role="status"` with `aria-busy`, bars hidden from assistive
technology, rows the height of real rows (40 px unless told otherwise) separated by a 1 px gap, sunken, no shimmer. It
registers the skeleton token set itself. Changes, the audit trail, the report (requirements at 96 px, the sheet while the
program loads), the register (both tabs) and People use it; the overview's composite skeleton and the screens of UI3-1
and UI3-3 are left to their items.

**D-513 — Changes: one sentence, then where it stands or its one action.** A rule change reads "Colorado rules changed from
2026 to 2026 revision 2", a course change "COUN 625 changed from catalog year 2026-27 to 2027-28", the subject in 500;
when a version has no name the sentence stays plain ("Colorado rules have a new version"). `versionPills` became
`versionDifference` (the same words, no pills). Between the sentence and the time sits one thing: the re-run as a row
action ("Re-run matching", accent text, named "Re-run matching, COUN 625, new catalog year"); "Blocked" or "Open" as a
term (`.lx-term`, focusable, `tooltipEvent="both"`, the reason in its tooltip and `aria-description`) when the person
cannot run it; "Handled" in meta when done; and while it runs its phase in meta (Queued, Re-running, Updating) with no
spinner, because nothing in v3 loops. The live message is screen-reader text; sighted people see the phase and the toast.
The title row's meta line keeps "2 open". What the change reopened and the two full version names open below the row.

**D-514 — Evidence report: one 816 px measure, the states as tabs, the downloads in the title row.** The sheet is 816 px
(US Letter at 96 dpi) on surface with the overlay elevation, 8 px radius and 56 px padding (24/20 under 640 px); the title
row and the state tabs share its measure, so the primary action ends where the sheet ends. The state Select became the
shared `StateTabs` row (as in Review and Coverage, without progress counts), so switching states is one click and the
title row holds only actions: Check a file and Download evidence package (secondary), Download PDF (the screen's primary)
and Help. The state name is set in Title type (Inter 24/32 600: the type table has no serif title), the sheet's meta in
meta type, requirement refs in Inter tabular 500, the quote in Document type on a 2 px line-strong rule, its source in
meta, the signature as a three-column definition list between hairlines. The sheet does not repeat "Evidence report" above
the state: the page title says it. The title row's meta line is the downloads' reason (D-515), alone, so the title, its
line and the four actions fit one row at the 816 px measure; under 640 px the actions wrap from the left. Page numbers are Inter, not mono (mono is for
hashes and offsets). The outcome word is ink in 600: the sheet carries no outcome colour, like the Seal.

**D-515 — The evidence package needs a ready report, which the screen knows only after generating one.** F15 builds a
package only for a signed determination whose report is READY (409 "Generate the report first" otherwise), and no
endpoint lists a determination's reports, so the screen knows a report is ready once Download PDF has finished for that
determination on this screen. Until then the package button is disabled and the title row's meta line says why, one line
for both downloads (`downloadsNote`, tested): "PDF and package after signing", "Reopened · PDF and package after signing
again" or "Package after the PDF"; the buttons name it as their description. Then the button requests the package
(`POST /determinations/{id}/package`), reads `GET /packages/{id}` every second (at most 120 times) while it reads
"Preparing package…", and hands `GET /packages/{id}/file` to the browser through a link, so the ZIP streams to disk and
is never held in memory; a "Package ready" toast follows. A later click downloads the same package again instead of
building another (every request is a new, separately audited package, D-540). A failed build shows the job's error.
Recommendation for the orchestrator: a `latestReadyReportId` on the determination (or a 404-free "latest report" read)
would let the screen offer the package to someone who generated the PDF yesterday; that is a backend change outside
this item, so this screen asks for the PDF again in a new visit.

**D-516 — Waiting is words, never a spinner.** PrimeNG's `[loading]` draws a rotating icon, a looping animation that
DESIGN.md forbids. Every button this item touches shows its state in its label instead ("Preparing PDF…", "Preparing
package…", "Loading…", the re-run phase) and is disabled with `aria-busy` while it waits; People's confirmation buttons
are disabled while busy instead of loading. Changes no longer imports ProgressSpinner.

**D-517 — The check dialog, finished.** F14's dialog keeps its behaviour and result texts. Its facts are Body text in
ink-2 (the local `.lx-meta` at 13 px, which shadowed the global meta class with a different size, is gone), "Open report"
is the shared row action, the fingerprint sits on a hairline with "SHA-256" in meta, the hash in mono and the copy
button (32 px) at the line's end, and the drop zone is a dashed line-strong frame with 40 px of air that takes the accent
edge while a file is over it. Every gap is on the 4 px grid. The dialog is appended to the body, outside the
component's host, so its earlier `:host ::ng-deep` rules for FileUpload's parts never applied (the upload's own framed
box showed around the dashed zone); they are now unscoped rules on the dialog's own class `lx-check-upload`.

**D-518 — Register: a table under a title row and a meta line.** The count left its Figure for the meta line ("1 signed
determination", still `register-count`); the tab's own action sits in the title row (Download CSV on Register, Print on
By program). The filters are one row of Selects named Program, Jurisdiction and Outcome with "All …" placeholders and
the Include reopened switch, like the audit trail's and People's. The table leads with the state (the link to its
report), then Program with its catalog year in ink-2, Outcome in found or missing words with a "Reopened" Label when it
is, Signed by, the signing day (the full time in its title), Rule version and the versions hash with its copy button; the
Status column is gone because every row but a reopened one says "Signed". Sub-lines are gone, so a row is one line where
the window allows. By program: three lists under headings on a line-strong rule, a signed row led by its 24 px Seal, a
row without a signature by nothing (the dashed placeholder circle is gone), the signature and rule version on one line.

**D-519 — People, setup, and what this item left out.** People's table is Name (with the You Label), Email in ink-2, Role
as a plain word (it has its own column, so it needs no Label), Status, Created and the row menu; avatars and the menu's
icons are gone (verbs only), so the screen registers no icons of its own. The count ("4 people", `message`) moved to the
title row's meta line. The setup page keeps the sign-in column (D-488) with its icons at 16 px, the strength word in
meta type and every gap on the grid. SEC-3 (PR #53) had not merged when this item started or when it merged main, so
the audit trail's "Verify" action is not built. The UI2-3 screenshot test, whose subject (timeline cards, the floating
pill) no longer exists, is replaced by the UI3-2 test; e2e selectors moved from the report's Select to `stateTab` and
from the "Change events" list to `changeRows` (a helper in `e2e/support.ts`). `goTo` in the same file now waits two
frames before asking whether a rail link is visible: right after a viewport change the shell moves its links on the
next frames, and the F14 screenshot test at 400 px timed out clicking a link that was visible when asked and hidden a
moment later (a shared helper, changed by two lines and called out in the pull request).
