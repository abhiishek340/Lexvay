# COUN 730 Clinical Supervision Seminar
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Elective course.

## Course description
This seminar prepares advanced students to receive supervision well and to begin to supervise others.
It is taken alongside the internship. Students bring cases from their placements, study the major supervision models and practice giving feedback.

## Learning outcomes
Describe the major models of clinical supervision and the roles of supervisor and supervisee.
Use supervision to learn from a difficult case and to manage the stress of clinical work.
Give a peer constructive feedback on a recorded session.

## Weekly schedule
Week 1 Purposes of supervision: development of the counselor, protection of clients and gatekeeping for the profession. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 2 The supervisory relationship. Power, trust, disclosure and what supervisees leave unsaid. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 3 Developmental models of supervision. The stages of a counselor's growth and what each stage needs. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 4 The discrimination model and the developmental model of clinical supervision, compared on a recorded case. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 5 Psychotherapy based models of supervision. Cognitive behavioral, psychodynamic and solution focused supervision. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 6 Contracts and evaluation. Writing goals, giving written feedback and handling a failing evaluation. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 7 Midterm case presentation from each student's placement. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 8 Legal and ethical issues. Vicarious liability, informed consent for supervision and records. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 9 Culture and difference in supervision. Raising difference openly. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 10 Group supervision and peer consultation. Reflecting teams. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 11 Burnout, compassion fatigue, self care and the supervisor's part in them. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 12 Supervising across modalities. Telephone and video supervision. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 13 Practice supervision of a classmate's recorded session, with feedback. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 14 Course review and supervision philosophy statements. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.

## Assignments and assessment
Case presentations (30%). Two presentations of a case from the student's internship, with a focus on a question for supervision.

Supervision philosophy statement (25%). A paper of 2,000 words describing the model the student would use to supervise.

Practice supervision (25%). A recorded fifteen minute supervision session with a classmate and a self evaluation.

Reflection logs (10%) and participation (10%).

Note for students on internship. Hours spent in this seminar do not count toward the internship hours reported in the clinical handbook.
