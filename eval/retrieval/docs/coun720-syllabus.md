# COUN 720 Counseling Children and Adolescents
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Elective course.

## Course description
Students learn play based and developmentally informed interventions for school age children and teenagers.
The course covers the developmental tasks of each stage, the role of parents and teachers in a child's treatment, and the legal questions that arise when a client cannot consent alone.
Laboratory sessions use toys, sand trays and expressive materials.

## Learning outcomes
Adapt the counseling process to the cognitive and emotional development of a child or teenager.
Select and justify an intervention for a child with a presenting behavior problem.
Describe how consent, confidentiality and reporting duties work when the client is a child.

## Weekly schedule
Week 1 Overview of child and adolescent counseling. The developmental perspective and the ecological model. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 2 Building the relationship with a child. Joining, limit setting and the physical set up of the playroom. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 3 Developmental assessment of children and adolescents. Interviewing parents, observing play and gathering school information. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 4 Child centered play therapy. Reflecting feeling, following the lead and structuring termination. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 5 Cognitive behavioral approaches for children. Worry, anger and the use of workbooks, stories and games. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 6 Expressive arts, sand tray and bibliotherapy in counseling. Ethical limits of interpretation. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 7 Midterm examination. Case conceptualization practice. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 8 Working with parents. Parent training programs, attachment concerns, and engaging reluctant caregivers. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 9 Adolescent development and identity. Peer relationships, social media and risk taking. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 10 Mood problems, self injury and school avoidance in adolescence. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 11 Neurodevelopmental differences in the classroom. Collaboration with teachers and with special education teams. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 12 Abuse, neglect and mandated reporting. Court involvement and the child's voice. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 13 Group work with children and adolescents. Bullying and prevention programs. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 14 Review and final case presentations. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.

## Assignments and assessment
Case conceptualization (30%). A written conceptualization of a child or teenager from a case study, with a treatment plan and a plan for working with the family and school.

Play session lab (25%). Two recorded sessions with a simulated child client, using approaches from weeks 4 to 6, each with a short reflective analysis.

Intervention portfolio (20%). Six activities for children of different ages, each with its purpose, materials, steps and cautions.

Midterm and final examinations (15%).

Participation (10%).

Texts. A child counseling textbook chosen by the instructor and the article collection.
