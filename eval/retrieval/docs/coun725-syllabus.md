# COUN 725 Counseling Skills Laboratory
Fictional. Prototype data. Northfield University does not exist.
Northfield University, M.S. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, 2026-27. 3 semester credits. Required course.

## Course description
A laboratory in the basic skills of the helping interview. Students practice attending, reflecting, questioning, summarizing and challenging in small groups, with immediate feedback from instructors and peers.
The laboratory meets for two and a half hours each week in the training clinic, where every session is recorded for review.

## Learning outcomes
Demonstrate the core skills of the helping interview in a supervised setting.
Respond to client emotion with accuracy and warmth.
Use feedback from recordings to set and meet personal skill goals.
---
## Weekly schedule
Week 1 Orientation to the training clinic, recording consent and the rules for practice sessions. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 2 Attending behavior and presence. Eye contact, posture, silence and the first five minutes. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 3 Open and closed questions. Avoiding interrogation and the why question. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 4 Reflection of content and paraphrase. Triad practice with counselor, client and observer. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 5 Reflection of feeling. Building a feeling vocabulary and checking accuracy with the client. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 6 Summarizing and structuring the session. Opening, middle and closing. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 7 Midterm skills check with an instructor, using the skills rating form. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 8 Immediacy, self disclosure and the use of the relationship in the room. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 9 Challenging and confrontation. Pointing out discrepancies with care. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 10 Goal setting and contracting. Turning concerns into workable aims. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
Week 11 Working with resistance, ambivalence and strong emotion in session. Reading: the assigned chapter and the case in the course packet. In class: case analysis in pairs, then a whole class debrief led by students.
Week 12 Ending sessions and ending the relationship. Referral conversations. Reading: the assigned chapter of the course text and one article from the course reader. In class: lecture followed by small group discussion of a case.
Week 13 Full length practice interviews with feedback from a faculty observer. Preparation: read the assigned chapter and post a short question on the learning site before class. In class: demonstration and guided practice in pairs.
Week 14 Final skills demonstration and course review. Reading: two articles from the course reader. In class: structured debate and a short reflection written before leaving.
---
## Assignments and assessment
Recorded practice sessions (40%). Each student submits three recorded practice sessions of twenty minutes, in weeks 5, 9 and 13. With each recording the student submits a transcript of five minutes, a skills rating and a one page reflection.

Skills checks (25%). The midterm skills check and the final demonstration, rated by an instructor on the departmental skills form.

Peer feedback (15%). Written feedback for classmates, graded on how specific and kind it is.

Learning contract (10%). Personal skill goals set in week 2 and revised in week 8.

Attendance and preparation (10%). The laboratory cannot be made up, so attendance is essential.

Texts. A microskills text chosen by the instructor.
